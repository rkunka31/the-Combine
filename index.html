League Info

charter · payouts · history · rules not in sleeper

Structure

12–14 teams · Sleeper · Dynasty · TEP scoring · $50/year · 2-year upfront

Roster

  • Starters: 1 QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, 1 TE, 2 FLEX = 9 (14-team) or 10 (12-team, +1 Flex)
  • Full: 9 starters (14-team) or 10 (12-team) + bench + 5 taxi + 5 IR
  • Taxi: 5 spots default · rookies and Year 2 only

Drafts

  • Startup: snake, random lottery order
  • Rookie: 3 rounds, MaxPF seeding for non-playoff teams
  • Picks tradeable up to 3 years; min 1 pick per round in next 2 drafts

FAAB & Trades

  • $100 FAAB · resets at training camp and offseason
  • Auto-approve trades · no veto · playoff trade restriction applies
  • Playoff teams can only trade with other playoff teams

Lineup Penalties

  • 1st: Warning · 2nd: $5 · 3rd: $10 · 4th: $20 · 5th: $40 FAAB
  • 6th+: $40 FAAB + loss of lowest rookie pick
  • Resets each season
